About & guide

About DRiShti-AI Studio

DRiShti-AI Studio is an explainable Diabetic Retinopathy screening and triage workbench built for rural Primary Health Centers (PHCs), combining image quality gating, interactive attention heatmaps, offline-first local storage, and Groq AI clinical guidance.

No DR No visible retinal damage — annual routine screening.
Mild Early microaneurysms — routine ophthalmology follow-up.
Moderate Noticeable retinal lesions — priority specialist referral.
Severe Extensive retinal damage — urgent specialist evaluation.
Proliferative DR Abnormal new vessels — immediate ophthalmology referral.

Clinical Disclaimer

DRiShti-AI Studio is an AI-assisted screening prototype. It does not replace professional ophthalmological examination, clinical judgment, or diagnosis by a qualified doctor.
